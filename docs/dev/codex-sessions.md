A help entry or schema method shows an available interface. It does not prove that an interactive role session can use
it.

## Missing needs by role and procedure

The measurements do not establish how an interactive planner or orchestrator starts a Codex session, names it, sets its
model or effort, sets its effective permissions, or reads its status. The procedures in
[`docs/dev/operator-start.md`](operator-start.md) and the role terms in [`docs/sessions.md`](../sessions.md) still
describe the existing flow. They do not have measured Codex session behavior to apply.

The tasker-run provider facts in [`docs/dev/assistants.md`](assistants.md) describe `codex exec`. They do not establish
interactive role-session behavior. Native team subagent results above also do not establish an independent role-session
address.

## Not measured and unresolved

- Native scheduled-job creation, firing, listing, and deletion did not run. The absence result is limited to the
  app-server schema and native team tool list.
- Queue delivery and wake behavior remain unmeasured across recipient states, target forms, and `--resume` settings.
  Queue acceptance, receipt, turn start, and reply are separate outcomes.
- Queue behavior for unavailable targets, ambiguous names, failed identity reads, and resume failures remains
  unmeasured. Complete discovery, pagination, and limits remain unmeasured.
- The task subagent prompt body was not supplied with the fact-test report.
- A pushed report after a background command ends remains unmeasured. Only polling was observed.
- Memory, repo-skill, and installed-skill discovery and invocation remain unmeasured. No memory note was written.
- Herdr interactive Codex start was rejected after one test tab was approved and closed. No test agent started.
- Session naming remains unresolved at Herdr start, native rename, same-UUID read-back, exact-name lookup, automatic
  Herdr binding, and replacement binding. The expected `planner-<slug>` and `orchestrator-<slug>` names remain
  unmeasured. No timing stage was measured.
- Interactive model and effort launch acceptance and first-call acceptance, including `max`, remain unmeasured.
- Effective interactive approval reviewer, policy, sandbox, and permission mode remain unmeasured. Help text does not
  establish these results.
- Interactive use of user rules and context-use status remains unmeasured.
- Automatic workspace selection remains unresolved until a measured match binds the Codex UUID to a pane and workspace.
  The inherited `wT` context is not such a match.
- The Herdr session row remains a partial observation because no `agent_session` UUID linked its Codex row to the shell
  UUID.
